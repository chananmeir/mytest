📚 How to Create Clothing Items & Generate Images

Creating Clothing Items

When adding a new clothing item, fill in all required fields. Each item needs a unique ID, display name, category, and slot assignment.

Generating Clothing Images with AI

Recommended Tools: Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion

Image Requirements:

  • Format: PNG with transparency (alpha channel)
  • Size: 512x512 to 1024x1024 pixels (consistent with character base images)
  • Background: Transparent - item should be isolated from background
  • Perspective: Front-facing, centered, as if worn on a body

AI Generation Tips by Category:

Underwear (Bras, Panties):

  • Prompt: "Front view of [color] [material] [item], isolated on transparent background, product photography, centered, high detail"
  • Example: "Front view of black lace bra, isolated on transparent background, product photography, centered, high detail"
  • Add descriptors: "delicate lace", "satin ribbon", "underwire", "push-up style"

Tops (Shirts, Blouses):

  • Prompt: "Front view of [style] [color] [type], flat lay product shot, transparent background, centered"
  • Example: "Front view of casual white t-shirt, flat lay product shot, transparent background, centered"
  • Specify details: "v-neck", "button-down", "sleeveless", "cropped"

Bottoms (Pants, Skirts):

  • Prompt: "Front view of [style] [color] [item], product photography, transparent background, symmetrical"
  • Example: "Front view of blue denim jeans, product photography, transparent background, symmetrical"
  • Include: "high-waisted", "pleated skirt", "mini/midi/maxi length"

Dresses:

  • Prompt: "Front view of [style] dress in [color], elegant product shot, transparent background, full length visible"
  • Example: "Front view of evening dress in red, elegant product shot, transparent background, full length visible"
  • Specify: "cocktail dress", "maxi dress", "A-line", "bodycon"

Post-Processing:

  • Use remove.bg or Photoshop to ensure clean transparent background
  • Remove any shadows or reflections from the floor/surface
  • Ensure item is perfectly centered in the canvas
  • Resize to match your character sprite dimensions

Coverage & Formality Guide:

  • Coverage 0-20: Thongs, bikinis, very revealing items
  • Coverage 20-40: Bras, panties, short skirts, crop tops
  • Coverage 40-60: T-shirts, jeans, casual dresses
  • Coverage 60-80: Long dresses, sweaters, conservative clothing
  • Coverage 80-100: Coats, full-body coverage
  • Formality 0-20: Gym wear, pajamas, loungewear
  • Formality 20-40: Casual everyday (jeans, t-shirts)
  • Formality 40-60: Smart casual (blouses, khakis)
  • Formality 60-80: Business attire (suits, dress shirts)
  • Formality 80-100: Formal wear (evening gowns, tuxedos)

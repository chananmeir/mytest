About Family Dynamics RPG

You are a 38-year-old unemployed individual at the bottom of your family's social hierarchy. Through the study and application of conversational hypnosis, you'll subtly shift the power dynamics within your family.

Core Mechanics

  • Rapport (0-20): Build trust through meaningful conversations
  • Emotional States: Observe and influence how family members feel
  • Suggestion Points (SP): Earn and spend to plant hypnotic suggestions
  • Hypnosis Learning: Study books, practice techniques, master the art
  • Post-Hypnotic Suggestions: Plant triggers that activate later

Learning Path

You must learn hypnosis techniques before you can influence family members. Study books, practice your skills, and master 11 techniques across 4 difficulty tiers to become a master of conversational hypnosis.
